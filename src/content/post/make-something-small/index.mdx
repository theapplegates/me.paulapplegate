There is a particular pleasure in making something that does not need to impress anyone. A sketch of a view. A recipe with one ingredient changed. A little website for things you want to remember.

## Choose a small beginning

Make the first version small enough to finish. One page, one paragraph, one afternoon. Constraints can give an idea somewhere to take shape.

## Follow what interests you

You are allowed to try a color because you like it. You can learn a tool just to see what it does. Curiosity does not need a business case.

## Keep what you learn

When you finish, write down what surprised you and what you might try next. A small project can leave you with something more useful than a polished result: another question worth following.
